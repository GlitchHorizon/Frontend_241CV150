Design Rationale-

The design of the website is inspired by modern Web3 platforms like Solana. I aimed to give the website a clean, futuristic, and minimal look while keeping it static for better performance and simplicity. The dark theme was chosen intentionally, paired with only two to three colors, to create balance.So, the website doesn’t look too plain or overly colorful.

The main goal was to solve the problem of the old, dull interface and bring in a fresh, modern touch. I reduced unnecessary information on the home page, like the side panel and credit section—because most students check their credits only once per semester. Instead, I focused on what students actually care about daily: attendance and grades.

To make this practical, I added an Attendance Card that shows how many classes are left and how many leaves a student can take. Since attendance is one of the most frequently checked items.

I also introduced a Reminders Section, allowing students to quickly save notes or upcoming deadlines right on the homepage. Every time they open the website, these reminders help them stay organized without needing an extra app.

The attendance display was redesigned from a straight bar to a circular progress section, which looks more interactive and visually engaging. Lastly, I added an Important Links Section, where students can personalize shortcuts, like “Pay Fees,” “Hostel Dashboard,” or any other pages they frequently visit. This makes the homepage cleaner, customizable, and more user-friendly.

Overall, my goal was to make the website modern, visually appealing, and practical, something students would actually enjoy using every day. It’s not just about design, but about making their daily digital routine smoother and more personal.
